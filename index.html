Gracious Hospital: Your Trusted Neuro and Dermatology Care in Mehdipatnam

Gracious Hospital, located in Mehdipatnam, Hyderabad, is recognized as the best neuro, dermatology, and cosmetology hospital specializing in neurological, skin, and aesthetic disorders. We understand that many conditions often go undiagnosed or are difficult to treat. Our expert team, led by Dr. M. A. Mukheem Mudabbir, a renowned neurologist, focuses on accurate diagnoses and comprehensive treatments for conditions like epilepsy, migraine, Parkinsons disease, stroke, and nerve pain.

Our dermatology and cosmetology departments, led by Dr. Nudrath Kahkashan, provide advanced treatments for psoriasis, acne scars, skin pigmentation, and cosmetic concerns. Whether you are searching for top neurologists or expert dermatologists and cosmetologists in Mehdipatnam, Gracious Hospital offers compassionate care and specialized treatments. Here, we believe that hope meets healing for every patient.

Our Services

Neurology Services

Neurodiagnostic Tests

Neurological Procedures

Clinical Dermatology

Comprehensive dermatology treatments for acne, pigmentation, infections & chronic skin conditions using advanced medical care.

Cosmetology Procedures

Dermatosurgery
