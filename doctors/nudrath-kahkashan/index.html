Meet your dermatologist

Thoughtful care for skin and aesthetic concerns

Dr. Nudrath Kahkashan is a consultant dermatologist, aesthetist and laser specialist at Gracious Hospital. Her practice includes dermatology and cosmetology, with care for a range of skin and aesthetic concerns.

Her listed qualifications include MBBS (Gold Medalist), MD-DVL (OSM) and Certified Aesthetist. Patients can contact the clinic to discuss appointment availability and the care options appropriate to their needs.

Care that starts with understanding your concerns

Skin conditions and cosmetic concerns can be different for everyone. An individual consultation helps clarify concerns and discuss suitable next steps.

Her areas of practice include psoriasis, acne scars, skin pigmentation and cosmetic concerns, alongside dermatology, aesthetic and laser services.
