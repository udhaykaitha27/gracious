Meet your neurologist

Experienced care for complex neurological needs

Dr. Mukheem Mudabbir is a neurologist with over 13 years of experience caring for adults and children. His clinical work includes common neurological concerns as well as complex and high-risk cases.

He completed medical training at Osmania Medical College and pursued specialist training in neurology at NIMHANS, Bengaluru. His approach brings careful assessment together with practical, individualised care planning.

Care that starts with listening

Neurological symptoms can affect everyday life in many ways. Consultation begins with understanding a patient's concerns and history, followed by an appropriate evaluation and discussion of care options.

His areas of practice include epilepsy, stroke, autoimmune neurological disorders, movement disorders, neuropathies, metabolic neurology, memory concerns and pediatric neurology.
